# Hubbell (HUBB): FY2025 research note

## Business
In its 10-K, Hubbell describes itself as follows: “We provide utility and electrical solutions that enable our customers to operate critical infrastructure reliably and efficiently, and we empower and energize communities through innovative solutions supporting energy infrastructure In Front of the Meter, on The Edge, and Behind the Meter.” [S20]

## Recent performance
FY2025 revenue grew 3.8% [S2] to $5,844.6m [S1], from $5,628.5m [S16] in FY2024. FY2025 net income was $887.1m [S6], or diluted EPS of $16.54 [S7]. FY2025 EBITDA was $1,414.9m [S8].

## Margins and returns
FY2025 gross margin was 35.3% [S3], up from 33.9% [S17] in FY2024. FY2025 EBIT margin was 20.7% [S5], up from 19.4% [S18] in FY2024. FY2025 ROIC was 18.7% [S9].

## Balance sheet and cash flow
At the end of FY2025 the company held cash of $482.5m [S11] against total debt of $2,325.4m [S12], giving net debt of $1,842.9m [S13]. FY2025 operating cash flow was $1,029.8m [S14] and capex was $155.1m [S15].

## Valuation versus peers
The shares closed at $461.10 [S19] on 2026-10-16. On 2026-10-16 prices, Hubbell trades at an FY2025 EV/EBITDA of 18.5x [S69], a discount to the peer median of 26.1x [S126]. Its FY2025 EV/Sales is 4.5x [S68] against a peer median of 5.0x [S127]. Its FY2025 P/E is 27.9x [S70] against a peer median of 35.4x [S128]. For context, its FY2025 EBIT margin of 20.7% [S5] is above the peer median of 15.8% [S129].
