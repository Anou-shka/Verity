# Vertiv (VRT): FY2025 research note

## Business
In its 10-K, Vertiv describes itself as follows: “Vertiv is a global leader in critical digital infrastructure for applications in data centers, communication networks, and commercial and industrial environments.” [S20]

## Recent performance
FY2025 revenue grew 27.7% [S2] to $10,229.9m [S1], from $8,011.8m [S16] in FY2024. FY2025 net income was $1,332.8m [S6], or diluted EPS of $3.41 [S7]. FY2025 EBITDA was $2,138.3m [S8].

## Margins and returns
FY2025 gross margin was 36.3% [S3], down from 36.6% [S17] in FY2024. FY2025 EBIT margin was 17.9% [S5], up from 15.3% [S18] in FY2024. FY2025 ROIC was 30.2% [S9].

## Balance sheet and cash flow
At the end of FY2025 the company held cash of $1,728.4m [S11] against total debt of $2,913.0m [S12], giving net debt of $1,184.6m [S13]. FY2025 operating cash flow was $2,113.8m [S14] and capex was $220.0bn [S15]. Organic orders in FY2025 were $4,784.6m [S16].

## Valuation versus peers
The shares closed at $206.42 [S19] on 2026-10-16. On 2026-10-16 prices, Vertiv trades at an FY2025 EV/EBITDA of 37.7x [S25], a premium to the peer median of 24.7x [S126]. Its FY2024 EV/Sales is 7.9x [S24] against a peer median of 4.9x [S127]. Its FY2025 P/E is 60.5x [S26] against a peer median of 33.3x [S128]. For context, its FY2025 EBIT margin of 17.9% [S5] is above the peer median of 15.8% [S129].

## Key risks (from the 10-K)
1. “We may not realize all of the sales expected from our backlog of orders and contracts.” [S21]
2. “Any disruption or consolidation of our customers’ markets or reduction in customer spending on technology could result in declines in the sales volume and prices of our products.” [S21]
3. “Larger customers often require terms and conditions that are more favorable to the customer, which could result in downward pricing pressures on our business.” [S21]
