# Eaton (ETN): FY2025 research note

## Business
In its 10-K, Eaton describes itself as follows: “Eaton Corporation plc (Eaton or the Company) is an intelligent power management company dedicated to protecting the environment and improving the quality of life for people everywhere.” [S20]

## Recent performance
FY2025 revenue grew 10.3% [S2] to $27,448.0m [S1], from $24,878.0m [S16] in FY2024. FY2025 net income was $4,087.0m [S6], or diluted EPS of $10.45 [S7]. FY2025 EBITDA was $6,179.0m [S8].

## Margins and returns
FY2025 gross margin was 37.6% [S3], down from 38.2% [S17] in FY2024. FY2025 EBIT margin was 18.8% [S5], in line with 18.9% [S18] in FY2024. FY2025 ROIC was 15.4% [S9].

## Balance sheet and cash flow
At the end of FY2025 the company held cash of $622.0m [S11] against total debt of $9,895.0m [S12], giving net debt of $9,273.0m [S13]. FY2025 operating cash flow was $4,472.0m [S14] and capex was $919.0m [S15].

## Valuation versus peers
The shares closed at $391.25 [S19] on 2026-10-16. On 2026-10-16 prices, Eaton trades at an FY2025 EV/EBITDA of 26.1x [S36], a premium to the peer median of 24.7x [S126]. Its FY2025 EV/Sales is 5.9x [S35] against a peer median of 4.9x [S127]. Its FY2025 P/E is 37.4x [S37] against a peer median of 33.3x [S128]. For context, its FY2025 EBIT margin of 18.8% [S5] is above the peer median of 15.8% [S129].

## Key risks (from the 10-K)
1. “We are subject to risks relating to acquisitions, joint ventures and investments, and risks relating to the integration of acquired companies.” [S21]
2. “Our operations depend on production facilities throughout the world, which subjects them to varying degrees of risk of disrupted production.” [S21]
3. “Significant inflation or shortages of raw materials, energy, components, and/or labor, or similar challenges for our customers, could continue to adversely impact our results of operations.” [S21]
